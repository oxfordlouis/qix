QIX

a 1981 arcade reconstruction

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Leave the border with FAST or SLOW held, draw an orthogonal line, and rejoin the border to claim land.
  2. The region without the Qix fills. Slow fill scores double; Fast fill is quicker.
  3. Reach 75% to clear a level. From level 3, splitting two Qixes also wins.
  4. Sparx patrol the edge. When the red Time Line runs out, more of them arrive.
  5. Stand still on an open line and a Fuse burns it. The Qix kills you if it touches that line.
  6. You cannot fly through empty space, cancel a line, or move diagonally.
